# Research & Paper Analysis Team

## Mission
Continuously discover, analyze, and synthesize relevant research to inform AI methodology decisions for the CAE-AI platform.

## Agents & Model Assignments
- **Paper Scout** (Haiku): Searches academic databases for relevant papers — routine keyword search
- **Paper Analyst** (Sonnet): Deep-reads papers, extracts methodologies and key findings
- **Literature Synthesizer** (Opus): Cross-references findings, produces synthesis reports — requires deep cross-domain reasoning

## Key Research Domains
- Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PINNs)
- Neural Operators (FNO, DeepONet, GNOT)
- Sim-to-Real Transfer Learning
- Graph Neural Networks for mesh-based simulation
- Multi-Fidelity Modeling
- Composite material ML
- Uncertainty Quantification
- Few-Shot Learning

## Outputs
- `research/papers/` — Paper summaries with methodology extractions
- `research/syntheses/` — Cross-paper analysis
- `research/recommendations/` — Prioritized methodology recommendations

## Tools
- WebSearch, WebFetch for paper discovery
- Read, Write for analysis documents

## Workflow
1. Scout searches by domain keywords
2. Analyst extracts: problem formulation, architecture, training strategy, metrics, limitations
3. Synthesizer produces actionable recommendations for AI/ML Team
