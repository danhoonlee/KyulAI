Research purpose

Why Double-Double laminate forecasting?

Double-Double laminates are being explored as lightweight, angle-driven alternatives to quasi-isotropic layups for impact and post-impact compression performance. This tool turns the simulation study into a fast screening interface: choose a Case and θ pair, then estimate Type, transition load Pt, response curve, and u3 behavior before committing to deeper analysis.

Problem Conventional 0/±45/90 stacks can add weight and limit design freedom.
Target Find the knee/transition point where force and u3 curves reveal stability loss.
Signal Current best DD candidates improved Pt by 28.93% and u3 metric by 31.31% vs. quasi-isotropic baselines.
How Type and u3 are used

Type 1 has a clear bilinear knee from force-displacement fits. Type 2 and Type 3 become more curved after the knee, so u3 displacement behavior is used to support transition-load estimation.
